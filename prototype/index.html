Momentum Lab

A top-down survivor arena where speed is the build. The map has real height, so hills, crests and landings change how fast you go. Your bolts hit harder the faster you move.

WASDArrows
Run.
Space
Jump. Press it again right as you land to bunny hop for +12% speed. Hold it to auto-hop and keep your speed without the boost.
Shift
Slide. Slides pick up speed downhill. Tightly packed contour lines mean steep ground.
Shift in air
Slam. Shockwave on impact, then slide out with a burst of speed.
Landing
Land on an enemy to stomp and bounce. Land on a downslope to turn your fall into speed.
Terrain
Yellow rings launch you. Hit a hill crest fast and you leave the ground.
